# Lightkeeper

Lightkeeper is a cinematic browser 3D arcade game about crossing a strange sci-fi star map, answering distress calls, and defending distant worlds one mission at a time.

Built around a Czech-flavored space fantasy, the game moves between free-flight travel and handcrafted planet encounters. One moment you are gliding through deep space toward a glowing beacon, the next you are carving through asteroid corridors, holding the line above a volcanic world, or stepping onto a planet surface to explore on foot.

## The Premise

You are a pilot of the Lightkeepers, guardians of a fractured system held together by fragile routes, isolated stations, and a single guiding signal in the dark. Every destination offers a different kind of challenge, and every launch feels like a jump into the unknown.

## Gameplay Pillars

- Fly freely across a stylized galaxy map and choose your next destination.
- Launch into fast arcade combat with corridor-style shooting missions.
- Defend threatened worlds in planetary defense scenarios with escalating pressure.
- Leave the cockpit for third-person surface exploration on select planets.

## Atmosphere

Lightkeeper aims for a clean low-poly look, neon sci-fi accents, and a sense of motion that feels inviting rather than harsh. The tone is adventurous, slightly mysterious, and designed to feel like an arcade voyage through a living star system.

Lightkeeper is fully client-side and built with Vite, A-Frame, and plain JavaScript ES modules.
